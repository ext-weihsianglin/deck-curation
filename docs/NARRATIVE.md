# Narrative rationale

The presentation answers four questions in order:

1. What did the assignment require, and what did the two-day effort produce?
2. What did the data support, and where did it limit the claims?
3. How did those constraints shape the extraction, scoring, and product decisions?
4. What does the working demo prove today, and what remains for a production path?

The deck avoids presenting classifier output as citation probability or causal uplift. It treats retention-first extraction as an engineering choice supported by benchmark evidence, not as proof of how proprietary answer engines ingest pages. The live demo shows the complete workflow while keeping mocked and deferred components visible.
